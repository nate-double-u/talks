## Mentor & community perspectives (added for India 2026)
- "What We Learned After Mentoring 30+ Mentees" — Arthur Sens (Grafana) & Bartłomiej Płotka (Google),
  KubeCon + CloudNativeCon EU 2026: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rUvwnbhn2ZQ
  (Key points: selection increasingly rests on interviews, not PR counts; AI-driven PR floods are a
  growing problem; "be brutal" with spammy contributors; mentees stay engaged via community/events.)
- Thanos & Prometheus Mentorship guide: https://thanos.io/tip/contributing/mentorship.md/
  (Key points: prefer public channels; participate in the project life cycle — conferences like
  KubeCon/PromCon, blogging, speaking; review others' PRs; be independent; iterate/POCs.)
